# Shot list

Record on the phone in **release** mode, portrait, Do Not Disturb on, brightness fixed, no notifications.
Screen-record at 60 fps (Android: built-in screen recorder; macOS: QuickTime → phone, or the macOS build in a phone-sized window).

## 1. Stadium tickets: widgets vs Flame (main perf video)
Clips are recorded separately, then put side by side in the edit (live split-screen would share one UI thread).
1. **Widgets, 20k:** open *Stadium tickets* → **Widgets** → **20k**. Turn on **Autopilot** (circle icon) and leave the **FPS** chip on.
   Record **12 s**. (Expect a few fps and visible stutter.)
2. **Flame, 20k:** switch to **Flame** → **20k** → Autopilot on. Record **12 s**. (Expect 60 fps.)
3. **Flame, hands-on (B-roll):** Autopilot **off**. Pinch-zoom into a block, tap 2–3 gold seats, watch "2 seats · ₹…" update in the bottom bar,
   then tap **Book tickets**. **15 s**.
- Edit: clips 1 and 2 side by side with labels "Flutter widgets" / "Flame", same start time. Optional: one line of text, "Same phone · 20,000 seats · 30 live bookings/s".

## 2. Savings reward: scratch card → coins into the vault (flagship)
1. *Savings reward* → **Claim**. Scratch slowly from the top-left; coins should start falling while you scratch.
2. Keep scratching until it reveals, let the coins pour, wait for **Collect ₹X**, tap it.
3. Result sheet "₹X cashback added".
- **One take, ~25–30 s.** Do 3–4 takes and keep the best one (some coins should miss the vault).
- The first 8 s of this clip doubles as the **cold open** of the talk.

## 3. Checkout moments (short montage, ~10 s total)
1. *Checkout moments* → **Add to cart** twice (product flies into the bag, badge bounces). **4 s**.
2. **Pay ₹4,999** → success screen: tick pops, cashback coins rain and pile up, counter climbs. **6 s**.

## 4. Robot vacuum: live home map (~20 s)
1. *Robot vacuum* → speed **4×**. Tap the **Kitchen** on the map: the route line appears, the robot turns on the spot and starts its stripes; the lidar sweep and point hits move with it. **6 s**.
2. Tap the **No-go** button (🚫), drag a rectangle across part of the kitchen still dirty, tap **Done**: the robot replans around it at once. **6 s**.
3. Tap the **follow** icon (target): the camera glides in behind the robot. Tap it again to zoom back out. **4 s**.
4. Tap **Home** (house icon): gold route back to the dock, status turns *Returning to dock* → *Charging*. **4 s**.
- Optional timelapse B-roll: **10×** → **Clean all**, record the whole house filling in (~60–90 s, speed it up in the edit).

## Tips
- Robot vacuum: tap any room on the map or its chip to clean just that room; Pause stops the robot where it is.
- Each screen restarts cleanly when you go back and open it again (fresh layout and seed).
- Stadium seat counts: 2k / 10k / 20k chips; the default is Flame at 20k.
- Turn the FPS chip off (speed icon) for the hands-on B-roll if you want a cleaner frame.
